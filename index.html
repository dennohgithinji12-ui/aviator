Game Rules & Fair Play Blueprint

1. Round Progression & Multiplier: Every round begins at a base multiplier of 1.00x and ascends exponentially until the aircraft flies away. Active stakes are multiplied by the cashout coefficient if collected prior to departure.

2. 97.0% RTP & Bust Gate: The game enforces a verified 97.0% Return to Player (3.0% House Edge). Under the Modulus Bust Gate, approximately 1 in 33 rounds (h % 33 == 0) terminates instantly at 1.00x before manual cashout can occur.

3. Cryptographic Provably Fair Architecture: Outcomes are calculated deterministically using HMAC-SHA256(Server Seed, Client Seed:Nonce). The first 13 hexadecimal characters (52 bits of entropy) map onto the continuous curve m = floor((100*e - h)/(e - h)) / 100. Operators cannot alter flight paths mid-round.

4. Deterministic Pre-Registered Auto-Cashouts: Auto-cashouts are registered on the authoritative server engine prior to takeoff and evaluated on the 50ms server clock. Payouts execute with zero latency penalty even if client connectivity drops mid-flight.

5. AML Compliance & Safe Payouts: To prevent financial abuse, withdrawals require 100% turnover of deposited funds (cumulative wagers ≥ deposits) and destination phone match.
