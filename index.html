How to Play Aviator?

Aviator is an exciting, provably fair multiplayer crash game. You play the role of an aviator, and your earnings are determined by how high you can fly the plane.

1

Place Your Bet

Select your bet amount and click BET before the lucky plane takes off. You can place two independent bets at the same time!

2

Watch Multiplier Rise

As the red plane climbs, the round payout multiplier starts at 1.00x and increases continuously.

3

Cash Out in Time

Hit CASH OUT before the plane flies away! Your win is your bet multiplied by the exact cash out coefficient.

⚡
Auto Bet & Auto Cashout

Set a target coefficient (e.g. 2.00x) to automatically cash out safely every single round.
